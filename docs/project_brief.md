# NeuroAccess: AI-powered EEG Literacy Platform

## One-Sentence Pitch

NeuroAccess helps non-expert users understand EEG data through multi-level AI explanations while preserving scientific and non-medical boundaries.

---

## Problem

**EEG data is difficult for non-experts to understand.**

Electroencephalography (EEG) is one of the oldest and most widely used brain imaging techniques, yet interpreting EEG recordings requires years of specialized training. Raw signals are unintelligible to students, educators, and curious learners. Frequency spectra and band power values are numerical abstractions without context. Clinical reports are dense and inaccessible.

The result: a fundamental neuroscience tool remains locked behind expertise barriers, preventing broader scientific literacy and public engagement with brain science.

---

## Solution

**The platform converts EEG signals into readable educational explanations for beginner, student, and research-level users.**

NeuroAccess bridges the gap between raw electrophysiological data and human understanding through a three-tier AI explanation system:

1. **Beginner (General):** Simple analogies and intuitive descriptions. Focus on what the data "means" in everyday language.
2. **Student (Learner):** Structured analysis with frequency band breakdowns, signal quality evaluation, and key EEG concepts.
3. **Research (Researcher/Tech):** Technical review with precise metrics, band ratios, artifact characterization, and methodological notes.

Each level is independently generated by a local LLM (Ollama + qwen2.5:7b) using level-specific prompts. The AI acts as a translator — not a diagnostician.

---

## Impact

**Improves scientific accessibility and lowers the barrier to neuroscience education.**

- Non-expert users can explore brain data without programming or math prerequisites
- Students gain structured, level-appropriate EEG analysis experience
- Educators have a ready-to-use demonstration tool for EEG concepts
- Researchers and learners can quickly assess signal quality and band characteristics
- The platform preserves scientific integrity while maximizing accessibility

---

## Technical Components

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| EEG Signal Processing | MNE-Python: loading, filtering, artifact detection, band power computation |
| Frequency Band Analysis | Delta (0.5-4Hz), Theta (4-8Hz), Alpha (8-13Hz), Beta (13-30Hz) power extraction |
| Signal Quality Scoring | Multi-factor scoring: noise, clipping, artifacts, channel quality |
| Local AI Explanation | Ollama + qwen2.5:7b: three independent, parallel explanation generation |
| Report Generation | Structured 8-section report with charts, scores, and explanations |
| PDF Export | dom-to-image-more + jsPDF: professional report export |

## Positioning

### Human-Centered AI

NeuroAccess embodies the principle that AI should serve human understanding, not replace human expertise. The three-level explanation system is designed around the user's cognitive level, not the model's capabilities. AI is the bridge between data and understanding.

### Scientific Accessibility

The platform removes barriers to EEG literacy: no programming, no math background, no specialized equipment required. A web browser and a local LLM are the only prerequisites.

### Neuroscience Education

NeuroAccess is fundamentally an educational tool. It teaches EEG concepts through interactive knowledge cards, curated case studies, and level-appropriate explanations. Every feature is designed to inform, not to diagnose.

---

## Non-Medical Boundary

NeuroAccess is not a medical device. It does not:
- Provide clinical diagnosis
- Offer treatment recommendations
- Replace professional medical interpretation
- Assess disease, intelligence, personality, or mental health conditions

This boundary is enforced at every level: system prompts, UI disclaimers, report structure, and public documentation.
